# The Butterfly Net (15 s)

Logline: Clawd wants to catch a butterfly, but every swing of the net misses, so Clawd gives up and sits still, and the
butterfly comes and lands on Clawd by itself.

World: one spring meadow under a big sky. Soft greens (sap, a paler far green), pale-blue hills, scattered rose, cream
and ochre flowers. The butterfly is cornflower blue with cream spots, so it reads against both grass and sky.
Colour arc: fresh, cool morning (pale blue sky) in A and B → warm, golden late light in C, as Clawd stops chasing and
calms down.

Motif: something landing on a flower. The butterfly lands on a pink flower in the first shot; Clawd crashes into a
flower bed in the second and comes up with a pink flower stuck on its head; in the last shot the butterfly lands on
that flower, on Clawd. Clawd has become the flower. (Ending rhymes with opening.)

Clawd's arc: bored → surprised → mischief → hopeful → determined → dizzy → sad → calm (eyes closed) → wonder → love.

Rhythm: 100 bpm.
